# Local Bazaar (Meri Local Dukaan) 🛍️

A complete All-India Hyperlocal Marketplace supporting both **Vercel Web App** and **Native Android App**.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **All 8 Categories**: Mobile & Laptop, Grocery & Produce, Local Services, Electronics, Bike & Scooty, Car & Auto, Personal Care, Fashion & Wear.
- **2–4 Photo Uploads**: Direct photo selection and real-time preview from device storage/gallery.
- **1-Click WhatsApp Chat**: Connect buyers directly with local sellers.
- **Location Selector & GPS**: Full database of All-India States and Districts with auto-detect.
- **Daily Mandi Rates & Community Requests**: Live community board and wholesale market rates.
- **Admin Moderation**: Account status management with User ID-based block/unblock controls.
- **Cart & Checkout**: Instant cart calculation and WhatsApp checkout.

## 🚀 Deployment
- **Web App (Vercel)**: Automatically deploys from `index.html` via `vercel.json`.
- **Android App**: Native Kotlin Jetpack Compose code located in `/app`.
